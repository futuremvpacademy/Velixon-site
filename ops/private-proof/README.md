# Private claim-support records

Files in this directory are internal substantiation records and are not deployed with the website.

- Do not copy these files into `public/`, marketing exports, shared issue trackers, or public repositories.
- Limit access to people who need to verify a published claim or respond to a dispute.
- Preserve the original source, capture date, full context, and any permission or material-connection record.
- Redact personal information before sharing with counsel or another provider unless the unredacted record is necessary and transferred securely.
- Remove a published testimonial if its accuracy, source, permission, or material-connection status cannot be confirmed.
