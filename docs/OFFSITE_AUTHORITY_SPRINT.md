# Velixon off-site authority sprint

Updated: July 15, 2026

This plan covers the work that turns Velixon's technical and content foundation into external authority. It is intentionally limited to truthful, relevant placements and first-party evidence. It excludes purchased links, fake reviews, bulk directories, keyword-swapped guest posts, and unverified partnership claims.

## Verified starting point

- Google Search Console reported one external link to `velixon.net`, from `ksl.com`, in the July 15 snapshot.
- The public site currently uses `Velixon.web@gmail.com`.
- `velixon.net` had no published MX record in the July 15 DNS check, so a working domain mailbox must be provisioned before the site or partner applications can safely publish `hello@velixon.net` or `partnerships@velixon.net`.
- The site can honestly use FormEsque and ShareCal as first-party product evidence. It must not describe them as independent client outcomes or invent adoption, revenue, time-saving, or ROI figures.

## First 30 days

### 1. Provision a domain mailbox

Owner action required: choose and provision a real mailbox or forwarding service at the current DNS provider. Verify both outbound delivery and replies before changing the public site.

Preferred public addresses:

- `hello@velixon.net` for inquiries
- `partnerships@velixon.net` for platform, media, and community outreach

Do not publish either address until the MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records have been configured and delivery has been tested.

### 2. Assemble the partner proof pack

Use these existing public assets:

- Company overview: `/about/`
- Delivery process: `/process/`
- FormEsque product evidence: `/case-studies/formesque-proposal-to-payment-platform/`
- ShareCal product evidence: `/case-studies/sharecal-collaborative-scheduling-platform/`
- Automation services: `/services/ai-automation/` and `/services/workflow-automation/`
- Platform implementation pages: `/integrations/n8n/`, `/integrations/zapier/`, and `/integrations/make/`
- Security approach: `/security/`
- Automation ROI tool: use the final canonical tool URL after deployment

Before any application, add only evidence that can be substantiated: screenshots, working demonstrations, code or architecture samples that are safe to publish, customer authorization, and measured outcomes with a stated period and method.

### 3. Apply to legitimate platform ecosystems

Priority order:

1. Zapier Solution Partner after the domain email is operational.
2. n8n Creator by publishing three tested, sanitized business-workflow templates.
3. Make Solution Partner when prior automation customer experience can be documented.
4. n8n Expert Partner only after the current customer requirement is genuinely met.
5. Twilio partner opportunities after production voice implementations can be demonstrated.

Never display a partner badge until the provider has approved the relationship and supplied usage rules.

### 4. Establish Utah prominence

- Submit a practical, non-promotional automation talk to relevant Silicon Slopes programming.
- Apply for a founder or practitioner interview only after the public expert identity and topic evidence are ready.
- Host a free Utah Tech Week workshop built around the live ROI/readiness tool and one real workflow teardown.
- Join only relevant Utah technology or business directories that review members and provide useful discovery beyond a link.
- Request coverage only for a real launch, original dataset, public tool, community event, or independently useful technical contribution.

### 5. Collect authentic third-party proof

- Create a complete Clutch profile using consistent company facts.
- Ask completed clients for an honest review without prescribing wording or offering incentives.
- Request permission before publishing a customer name, logo, quote, screenshot, result, or system detail.
- Link each approved case study from the client's site when the customer believes it is useful to their audience.

## Original authority asset

The strongest research concept is a `Utah SMB Automation Readiness Benchmark` based on a genuine survey of Utah service businesses and contractors.

Required before publication:

- defined eligible population and collection period;
- transparent questions and methodology;
- minimum sample threshold established before analysis;
- anonymization and consent rules;
- raw or aggregated data that can be safely shared;
- charts with denominators and uncertainty;
- no claims that exceed the data.

Until enough responses exist, publish only the participation methodology or keep the study in collection. Do not manufacture a benchmark from hypothetical inputs.

## Weekly operating cadence

- Earn or pitch one legitimate authority opportunity.
- Improve or distribute one first-party evidence asset.
- Record contact, target page, status, follow-up date, and outcome.
- Review new Search Console links and referring domains.
- Reject placements whose primary value is a paid ranking link.

## Success measures

- new relevant referring domains, weighted by relevance and editorial legitimacy;
- partner or creator profiles approved;
- authentic reviews published;
- qualified referral traffic to commercial and proof pages;
- non-branded Search Console impressions and queries;
- organic inquiries attributed to the landing page and source;
- citations or mentions of Velixon products, tools, research, or expertise.

The business outcome is qualified pipeline and defensible market authority, not a target Domain Rating or a raw backlink count.
