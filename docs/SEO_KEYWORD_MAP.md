# Velixon Phase 1 keyword map

Updated: July 15, 2026

## Purpose

This map assigns one principal URL to each commercial or informational intent. It is a planning and governance document, not evidence that Velixon currently ranks for any query.

Status definitions:

- **Existing**: the route is present in the audited repository.
- **Created**: the route was added during the current Phase 1 implementation. Update a row from Planned only after the route exists and passes QA.
- **Planned**: required for Phase 1 but missing at the time of the repository audit.
- **Deferred**: preserve for Phase 2 or later; do not publish an indexable page until it has distinct evidence and passes the unique-value gate.

## Canonical ownership rules

1. The homepage owns the Velixon entity and broad category proposition. `/services/ai-automation/` owns the exact commercial intent **AI automation agency**.
2. Keep the established short service URLs. Do not create competing pages at `/services/custom-software-development/` or `/services/custom-crm-development/`; redirect those aliases to `/services/custom-software/` and `/services/custom-crm/` if they are ever requested.
3. `/services/ai-automation/` covers model-assisted interpretation and action. `/services/workflow-automation/` covers deterministic orchestration. `/services/business-process-automation/` covers organization-wide process redesign.
4. The existing `/integrations/` architecture is the canonical technology and platform cluster. Do not duplicate it under `/technologies/`. Redirect `/technologies/n8n/`, `/technologies/make/`, and similar aliases to their `/integrations/` equivalents if needed.
5. `/services/ai-integrations/` should explain how AI capabilities are embedded in business systems. `/services/api-integrations/` remains the broader system-to-system API engineering page. `/integrations/openai/` remains platform-specific.
6. Contractor, service-business, and construction pages must remain distinct: contractor intent centers on estimating and job operations; service-business intent centers on intake, scheduling, dispatch, and recurring service; construction intent centers on project delivery across office and field roles.
7. Location pages are service-area pages, not office pages. They must not imply an address, proximity, or local employees unless verified.
8. Existing comparison and guide pages retain the informational intent named below. New resources must link to them rather than restating them.
