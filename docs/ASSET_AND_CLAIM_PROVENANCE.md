## Claim rules

- Publish no customer, revenue, sales, savings, lead, ranking, uptime, security, compliance, or delivery claim without dated supporting evidence.
- Do not describe a review as “verified” unless the verification method is accurate and documented.
- Do not reuse a related-party, employee, relative, contractor, investor, or incentivized endorsement without a clear, conspicuous material-connection disclosure approved for that placement.
- Remove or correct a testimonial if the source is deleted, materially edited, disputed, or no longer accurately excerpted.
- Keep calculator defaults and examples labeled illustrative. Preserve formula definitions and source dates.
- Record licenses for stock media, icons, music, fonts, templates, and generated assets before publishing them.

## Release record

For every new public asset or claim, record:

1. Public URL and exact wording or file hash.
2. Owner/creator and source.
3. License, assignment, consent, or applicable permission.
4. Evidence supporting express and implied claims.
5. Material connections or incentives.
6. Reviewer and approval date.
7. Expiration or revalidation date, if applicable.
