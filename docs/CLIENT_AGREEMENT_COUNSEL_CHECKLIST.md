# Client agreement counsel checklist

This is an issue list for a licensed attorney, not a contract and not legal advice. Do not copy generic internet clauses into a client agreement without review.

- Correct legal names, addresses, signing authority, and effective date
- Services, deliverables, exclusions, milestones, acceptance, and client dependencies
- Change-request procedure and effect on fees and schedule
- Fees, deposits, expenses, taxes, invoicing, late payment, suspension, and refunds
- Client-provided content, data, permissions, legal compliance, and third-party accounts
- Confidentiality, approved communication channels, and security responsibilities
- Personal-data roles, processing instructions, subprocessors, retention, deletion, and incident notice
- Background technology, client deliverables, open-source software, licensed assets, portfolio rights, and intellectual-property transfer timing
- AI-specific inputs, outputs, human review, provider terms, prohibited data, and output limitations
- Accessibility scope and responsibility for content or third-party changes
- Warranties, disclaimers, service levels, maintenance, support, backups, and disaster recovery
- Indemnification, liability exclusions, liability cap, insurance, and claims procedure
- Term, termination, transition assistance, data export, deletion, and surviving provisions
- Governing law, venue, negotiation or mediation, arbitration if appropriate, and fee allocation
- Electronic signatures, notices, assignment, subcontractors, force majeure, severability, waiver, and entire agreement
