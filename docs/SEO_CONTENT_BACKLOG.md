# Velixon Phase 2 content backlog

Updated: July 15, 2026

## Scope

Every item in this document is **deferred to Phase 2 or later**. None of these ideas should be published during Phase 1. They are a research backlog, not a quota, and they should not be generated in bulk.

The three Phase 1 starter resources are intentionally excluded from the backlog count:

1. What Is Business Process Automation?
2. How Much Does AI Automation Cost?
3. How to Know When Your Business Needs Custom Software

## Selection and publication rules

Promote a backlog item only when at least one of these signals exists:

- Search Console shows relevant impressions, adjacent queries, or a content gap on an existing page.
- A qualified prospect repeatedly asks the question during discovery or sales conversations.
- Velixon can contribute first-party implementation evidence, a screenshot, a tested workflow, a calculator, a template, a benchmark from its own systems, or a named practitioner insight.
- The article materially supports a verified service, product, case study, or integration capability.
- Current search results reveal an answer Velixon can improve with clearer technical judgment, operational detail, or a more useful decision tool.

Every published resource must have a distinct intent, answer the query directly, cite current primary sources when factual claims depend on external information, identify Velixon as the author or use a verified team member, and disclose limitations. Legal, compliance, tax, healthcare, or financial topics require qualified review and must not be framed as professional advice.

## Backlog QA gate

Before moving any item into production, confirm:

- Its primary intent is not already owned by an existing page in `docs/SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md`.
- The article adds something materially more useful than a prose summary of existing search results.
- Claims, dates, product behavior, laws, prices, and platform limits are verified against current primary sources.
- Any calculation exposes its formula, assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations.
- The page has contextual links to one parent service, one related supporting page, and a relevant next step.
- The article uses Article schema that matches visible authorship and dates.
- No client, result, credential, ranking, office, testimonial, or statistic is fabricated.
- The page passes build, metadata, structured-data, accessibility, internal-link, and mobile QA before sitemap inclusion.
